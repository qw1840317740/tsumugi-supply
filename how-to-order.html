Ordering

How to order in four steps

Build a quote list or send a product request. We confirm availability, order minimums and freight before you decide to buy. No account or payment is needed to inquire.

Minimum order applies — quoted on request
1

Build your quote list

Add products from the catalog, then send your quantities and contact details.

Choose your destination in the inquiry form. Shipping is confirmed with your quote.
2

Get your invoice

We email a proforma invoice with shipping within 1 business day.

Orders over 30 kg are re-quoted with exact freight before payment.
3

Pay by bank transfer

Bank wire transfer (T/T) only. No card or PayPal.

For T/T bank transfers, both sides' bank charges are borne by the buyer.
4

We ship

Availability and the dispatch schedule are confirmed in your quotation before payment.

3+ cartons of a single SKU may take longer — we confirm timelines upfront.

Shipping & fees

Two ways to receive your order

EMS air freight

Fastest option — tracked, insured, 3–10 days to most destinations. Ideal for time-sensitive or high-value shipments under 30 kg.

From ~¥2,800 / kg (varies by zone)

Consolidated sea freight

Best landed cost for larger orders. We consolidate multiple brands into one shipment, 2–5 weeks transit, full documentation.

Best value from ~¥50,000 order

Final freight is confirmed in your proforma invoice. Duties and taxes are the buyer’s responsibility unless arranged otherwise.
