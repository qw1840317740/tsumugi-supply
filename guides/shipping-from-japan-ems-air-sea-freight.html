Shipping Wholesale Orders from Japan: EMS vs Air Freight vs Sea Freight

How the main shipping methods for Japanese wholesale orders compare on speed, cost and suitability, plus what to know about liquids, aerosols and bulky items.

Published: 2026-10-23

Parcel services (EMS and courier)

Japan Post EMS and international express couriers are the simplest option for sample and small orders. They are fast and door-to-door, but cost rises quickly with weight. Japan Post EMS accepts parcels up to 30 kg each, so a larger order is split into several parcels.

Air freight

Air cargo suits medium-sized or time-sensitive orders. Charges are based on the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight, so light but bulky goods such as diapers or tissues can be expensive by air. Customs clearance at destination is usually arranged by the buyer or their forwarder.

Sea freight: LCL and FCL

For larger and heavier orders, sea freight is usually the most economical. LCL (less than container load) shares a container with other shippers and is charged by volume; FCL (full container load) gives you a whole 20 ft or 40 ft container. Transit takes longer than air, so plan stock accordingly.

Heavy liquids such as laundry detergent, fabric softener and dish soap are typical sea-freight items.

Liquids, aerosols and alcohol

Aerosol sprays, alcohol-based hand sanitizers and some foggers and fragrances can be classified as dangerous goods, which limits or prohibits air shipment and may require special handling by sea. Tell us the destination early so the shipping method can be checked item by item.
