Japanese Refill and Pack Sizes Explained: Tsumekae, Tokudai, Jumbo and More

Japanese household brands sell bottles plus refills in sizes from regular to “ultra jumbo”. A glossary of the size words and how to plan wholesale orders around them.

Published: 2026-10-23

Main unit vs refill

本体 (hontai) is the main unit: a bottle with a pump, cap or trigger. つめかえ / 詰替 (tsumekae) is a refill pouch meant to be poured into that bottle. Refills use less packaging and are usually cheaper per millilitre, which is why Japanese shoppers buy them in large numbers. A typical shelf for one product line carries one bottle and several refill sizes.

Size words you will see

大 / 大型 (large), 特大 (tokudai, extra large), 超特大 (super extra large), ジャンボ (jumbo), 超ジャンボ (super jumbo), ウルトラジャンボ (ultra jumbo), メガジャンボ (mega jumbo), and on some laundry-pod lines ハイパージャンボ and テラジャンボ. 増量 means a temporary bonus-size pack, and “4回分” on a refill means it holds four bottle-fills.

These words are relative to each brand’s own regular size, not fixed volumes. Always check the millilitres, grams or piece count tied to the JAN.

Planning a wholesale order

A practical mix is one bottle SKU to introduce the product plus one or two refill sizes for repeat customers. Large refills give the best price per millilitre but the case packs are heavy, so they make the most sense in sea-freight orders. For air shipments, regular refills or bottles are often easier.
