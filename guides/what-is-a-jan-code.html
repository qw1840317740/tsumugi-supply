What Is a JAN Code? Why Wholesale Buyers Should Order Japanese Products by JAN

JAN codes are the Japanese form of the GTIN barcode. Learn how to read them and how ordering by JAN prevents wrong sizes, scents and package versions.

Published: 2026-10-23

JAN is Japan’s GTIN

A JAN (Japanese Article Number) is the barcode number printed on products sold in Japan. It belongs to the same global GTIN system as EAN barcodes in Europe and UPC in North America. Most JAN codes have 13 digits; small items sometimes carry a short 8-digit version.

JAN codes issued in Japan usually start with 45 or 49, the prefixes allocated to GS1 Japan.

Why the product name is not enough

A single product line can have dozens of JANs. In our catalog, one fabric-softener line alone appears in different scents and in bottle, refill, extra-large and super-extra-large sizes, each with its own JAN. Names in English or Chinese translations can also differ between suppliers, while the JAN on the package stays the same.

Ordering by JAN removes that ambiguity: the code identifies the exact size, scent, shade and package format.

Renewals and successor JANs

When a manufacturer changes a formula or package, the item may receive a new JAN and the old one is discontinued. If a JAN you sold before is no longer available, ask for the successor item and compare its size and contents before reordering.
