Frequently asked questions

Everything about pricing, payment, shipping, authenticity and after-sales. Can’t find your answer? Message us by phone or email.

Do I need to pay to request a quote?

No. Building a quote list and submitting an inquiry is free. We confirm pricing and freight before you decide to proceed.

What is the minimum order amount?

The order minimum and per-product requirements are confirmed in your quotation. You can submit a free inquiry for any quantity; there is no payment or minimum spend to send a request.

Are all products authentic and sourced in Japan?

Yes. Every item is sourced through authorised Japanese distribution channels. We verify serial and lot codes and can provide documentation on request.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank wire transfer (T/T via SWIFT) only. Both banks' SWIFT charges are borne by the buyer. We do not accept PayPal or card.

How is shipping calculated?

Shipping depends on destination, total weight and shipping method. We confirm freight in your proforma invoice before payment.

How long until my order ships?

The dispatch date depends on availability, sourcing and shipment size. We confirm the schedule in your quotation before payment. See How to Order for the published shipping terms.

Can I get a better price on large volumes?

Yes. Pricing is tiered by volume, and we quote bespoke pricing on 5,000+ pieces per SKU. Contact us by phone or email for a bulk quote.

Can you consolidate multiple brands into one shipment?

Yes — consolidation is core to what we do. We combine multiple brands and categories into a single air or sea shipment to lower your landed cost and simplify customs.

What if a product is out of stock or renewed?

We always ship the latest available packaging and notify you of any product renewals or discontinuations. If a requested item is unavailable, we suggest the closest equivalent before invoicing.

Can I become an exclusive distributor?

Possibly. We work with exclusive distributors in select markets. Tell us about your business and territory via the product request form and our team will follow up.
